## Rebuilding MSP

MSP had stalled. Execution had drifted and the chapter needed a reset to train working engineers rather than event staff.

I stepped in alongside friends I trusted to lead. We formed a tight board, replaced vague roles with clear ownership, and set engineering rules from day one.

![MSP Openning Group Photo](/images/msp-openning.jpg)

## Launching Resonance

I built **Resonance** as an AI committee focused on sound, acoustics, and frequency analysis. The goal is to turn curious students into audio AI engineers who understand physical sound before touching large models.

## Board and Engineering Standards

My co-leads and I established a clear chain of command so decisions move fast. We adopted two mandatory technical rules from the start: version control through GitHub and a Unix or Linux development environment to cut out Windows-specific debugging issues.

## The Five-Month Plan

With the board set, we launched a five-month curriculum split into two phases:

**Phase 1: Audio Engineering Foundations**
We start from the physics of sound, spectrograms, and the Short-Time Fourier Transform. Within two months, the team will build a working **Shazam-style audio fingerprinting system** from scratch.

**Phase 2: Deep Learning and Piano Transcription**
Once the DSP fundamentals stick, we move into PyTorch and neural networks. The season capstone is a **Piano Transcription Model** that reads raw audio, outputs MIDI, and feeds a digital piano simulator in real time.

## Two-Tier Recruitment

We structured hiring in two waves. First, Open Enrollment welcomes motivated students with basic computer skills so we can spot strong learners early. Second, a targeted recruitment wave brings in members who already know Python and machine learning ahead of the deep learning phase.

## Multi-Year Roadmap

Beyond the first five months, I mapped out three long-term tracks for Resonance:
* **Year 2:** Train an Egyptian Arabic Text-to-Speech model to address the gap in local dialect support.
* **Audio Engineering:** Build adaptive spatial audio systems modeled on Dolby Atmos.
* **2027 Goal:** Combine audio AI with radio frequency engineering to build a transceiver that can contact the International Space Station for the NASA Space Apps challenge.

Resonance is laying the groundwork for audio AI at FCIS, one late-night build at a time.
