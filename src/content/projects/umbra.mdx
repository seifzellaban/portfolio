# Umbra

Umbra was built for the **2025 NASA Space Apps Challenge**. It empowers researchers, mission planners, and citizen scientists to explore, filter, and visualize NASA’s vast library of space biology experiments.

## The Challenge

NASA has an incredible amount of data from decades of space biology research, but it's often fragmented and difficult for the general public or even researchers to navigate quickly.

## Our Solution

We built Umbra to provide a seamless, modern interface for this data. Key features include:
- **3D Visualizations** of space biology datasets.
- **Advanced Filtering** by organism, experiment type, and flight mission.
- **Interactive Timelines** of research milestones.

## Impact

Umbra enabled researchers to visualize over **600+ space biology experiments** in a cohesive manner, fueling the next era of human space exploration.
